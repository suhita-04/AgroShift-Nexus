The Mission Framework

Translating Orbit Data into Ground Resilience

NASA’s Earth-observing satellites provide diagnostic metrics that take the guesswork out of drought survival and climate adaptation.

SMAP Root Soil Moisture

Using spaceborne microwave radiometry to measure root-zone water content down to 100cm, pinpointing flash droughts days before crops show visual wilting.

Predictive Crop Adaptation

Replacing vulnerable monocultures with climate-resilient alternatives (Sorghum, Pearl Millet, Quinoa) tailored specifically to regional rainfall variance.

ECOSTRESS Evapotranspiration

Thermal infrared sensing from the International Space Station guides precise night-time drip cycles to eliminate up to 40% of daytime evaporative losses.
